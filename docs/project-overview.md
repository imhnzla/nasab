# Project Overview

## What is NASAB?

**NASAB** (نسب — "lineage") is a non-profit digital platform for preserving, verifying, and visualising the lineage of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). It provides a single authoritative source that connects historical genealogical records with living descendants worldwide.

## Vision

To become the world's most trusted, inclusive, and scholarly genealogical platform for the lineage of the Prophet — accessible to all Muslims regardless of sect, school of thought, or geography.

## Mission

Digitise, verify, and connect the Shajra Nasab (family tree) of the Prophet with rigorous scholarly standards, making it freely accessible to researchers, descendants, and the general public.

## Core Principles

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Non-discriminatory** | Sunni and Shia lineage traditions treated with equal rigour; both shown side-by-side where they differ |
| **Source-cited** | Every person node requires at least one scholarly source |
| **Verification-first** | No lineage claim added without documented proof |
| **Non-profit forever** | No monetisation of genealogical data |
| **Bilingual** | Arabic (canonical) + English (transliteration) throughout |

## Target Audience

1. **Sayyids and Sharifs** — descendants seeking to document and connect their lineage
2. **Islamic scholars and researchers** — academic verification and citation
3. **General Muslim public** — exploring the Prophet's family history
4. **Institutional bodies** — Ashraf organisations, Islamic universities, waqf bodies

## What NASAB Is Not

- Not a social network
- Not a commercial genealogy service
- Not a platform for unverified family claims
- Not affiliated with any government, political movement, or religious institution
